RICARDO DANIEL ALVARADO
A PRACTICE IN STRUCTURAL INTENSITY.

Architecture is rarely a silent pursuit. As a designer, I am not interested in quiet power or polite restraint. I am drawn to structural intensity, technical noise, and the complicated intersections of fabrication and systems. Whether working with physical materials or digital spaces, my work embraces the friction of making.

THE PHYSICAL FOUNDATION.

Grounded by a family history in the construction industry, my path to architecture was built on a tangible understanding of how things are put together. I am currently a Master of Architecture candidate at the University of Pennsylvania Weitzman School of Design, having earned my undergraduate degree in architecture from Washington University in St. Louis.

My studio work heavily explores biocomposite materials, digital fabrication methods, and 3D printing with bark-based composites. From my roots in Southern California to my current base in Philadelphia, I am deeply interested in speculative urbanism and how we densify our physical environments.

THE DIGITAL LANDSCAPE.

Distinct from my physical architectural practice is an ongoing exploration of the digital realm. Through creative coding, interactive design with p5.js, and an interest in the history of the web, I build spaces that explore digital autonomy.

This portfolio serves as an archive for these specific digital explorations. It is a space where my interests in avant-garde pop aesthetics, and messy internet vernacular collide to form new, interactive experiences outside the traditional studio environment.
